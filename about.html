The Pulse of the 254

Central Texas is growing fast. 254 Texas Insider keeps you ahead of it.

What we cover

Business and development news from Waco, Temple, Killeen, Belton and everywhere in between: the new builds, the openings, the land deals and the decisions at City Hall that shape where the 254 is headed. Plus schools, high school sports, things to do, local history and plain-English explainers on how local government works.

How we report

We read the city agendas, county filings, school district announcements and local outlets so you don't have to, then explain what it means for you. Every story credits and links to the original reporting and public records it draws on.

Who we are

254 Texas Insider was founded by Jake, a Temple-based business professional who works with facility and business leaders across Central Texas every day. It started as daily videos on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook and has grown into a full news site and podcast.
