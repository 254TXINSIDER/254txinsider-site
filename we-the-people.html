Who decides what in the 254

City council. Your city council sets the city budget and tax rate, approves zoning and development, and oversees services like police, fire, streets, water and parks.

Commissioners court. Every Texas county is run by a commissioners court — a county judge plus four commissioners elected by precinct. Waco is in McLennan County; Temple, Killeen and Belton are in Bell County.

School board. Elected trustees set the district budget and tax rate, call bond elections, and hire the superintendent.

How to be heard. Most of these meetings are open to the public, post their agendas in advance, and set aside time for public comment. We the People will tell you what's on the agenda before the vote, not after.
